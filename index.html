Who is GearShift Robotics?

GearShift Robotics is a rookie FIRST Tech Challenge robotics team based in Milpitas, California. Every season, we design, build, and program a brand-new robot from scratch to take on that year's game challenge.

Our team currently has 10 members working across hardware, software, and project management. Our hardware team designs and builds the robot, our software team writes the code for vision, odometry, and outtake, and our project managers keep everything organized and on schedule.

As a first-year team, we're learning something new at every meeting — including how to make mistakes and learn from them together. We're excited to grow as engineers and teammates, compete in the FIRST Tech Challenge, and share what we learn with our community.
